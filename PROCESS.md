The scrum process we'll be following is as follows -
We will have a scrum master who will lead our team for the sprint duration using agile project management.
We will have a product owner who will create a vision of the product they wish to create and communicate that product vision to the team. While this will also last for a sprint, our more permanent product owner is Christine.
We will abide by our definition of "done" when working on our stories from our story and product backlogs. We will ensure that we create achievable stories within a sprint.
We will also use metrics to see our success rates in finishing our tasks.
We will test any updates for errors before pushing to the GitHub repository to prevent the product from failing.
By the end of the semester, we hope to have a working product that goes above and beyond in supplying the "clients" of Lewis University with a product they can personally use and rely on to enhance their academic experience.
We will make sure to demo our product to one another and continue to show progress by completing personally assigned tasks.
