The scrum process we'll be following is as follows -
We will have a scrum master who will lead our team for the duration of a sprint using agile project management.
We will have a product owner who will create a vision of the product they wish to create and communicate that product vision to the team. While this also will last for the duration of a sprint, our more permanent product owner is Christine.
We will abide by our definition of "done" when working on our stories from our story and product backlogs. We will make sure that we create stories that are achievable within a sprint.
We will also use metrics to see our success rates in finishing our tasks.
By the end of the semester, we hope to have a working product that goes above and beyond in supplying the "clients" of Lewis University with a product they can personally use and rely on to enhance their academic experience.
